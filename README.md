# Awesome-Industrial-Connected-Frontline

## Top Industrial Connected Frontline Platforms Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on Connected Worker, Digital Work Instructions, Frontline Collaboration, Skills, Maintenance & Shop-Floor Execution*

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Industrial Connected Frontline** (connected worker) solutions. These systems deliver digital work instructions, capture tribal knowledge, support skills and training, enable frontline collaboration, and connect operators, technicians, and supervisors on the plant floor.



**Examples** include Augmentir, Poka, Parsable, MaintainX, Tulip, REWO, Dozuki, Operations1, Beekeeper, and WorkJam (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Full connected-frontline platforms are mostly commercial. Practical open options include **digital work-instruction prototypes**, **AR guidance experiments**, open CMMS building blocks, wiki/SOP tools, and collaboration platforms adapted for industrial use. This section lists the strongest available open resources and is realistic about the gap.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[Augmentir](https://www.augmentir.com/)**  

  AI-powered connected worker platform delivering personalized work instructions, skills insights, and remote support for industrial frontline teams.



- **[Poka](https://www.poka.io/)**  

  Connected worker platform combining digital work instructions, skills management, checklists, and frontline engagement for manufacturers.



- **[Parsable](https://www.parsable.com/)**  

  Connected worker and digital procedure platform focused on standard work, execution tracking, and operational excellence on the shop floor.



- **[MaintainX](https://www.getmaintainx.com/)**  

  Mobile-first work order and maintenance platform widely used by frontline maintenance and operations teams for asset care and collaboration.



- **[Tulip](https://tulip.co/)**  

  No-code frontline operations platform that lets teams build apps for work instructions, data capture, and machine connectivity without traditional coding.



- **[REWO](https://www.rewo.io/)**  

  Digital work instruction and knowledge-capture platform focused on visual standard work and shop-floor guidance.



- **[Dozuki](https://www.dozuki.com/)**  

  AI-powered connected worker platform focused on digitizing standard work, version control, training, and knowledge sharing—strong in regulated environments.



- **[Operations1](https://operations1.com/)**  

  Connected worker and digital work instruction platform for structured shop-floor processes, quality, and knowledge management.



- **[Beekeeper](https://www.beekeeper.io/)**  

  Frontline communication and engagement platform that connects deskless and industrial workers with operational updates and collaboration tools.



- **[WorkJam](https://www.workjam.com/)**  

  Digital workplace platform for frontline and deskless workers covering scheduling, communication, tasks, and engagement.

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- Building standard work in **open wikis or document systems** with strict version control and approval workflows.

- Using **open CMMS** components for maintenance-heavy frontline use cases.

- Experimenting with **open AR work-instruction prototypes** for high-value assembly or maintenance tasks.

- Combining open procedures with commercial connected-worker platforms when scale and AI guidance are required.

- Accepting that polished connected-worker experiences, AI-assisted authoring, skills analytics, multi-site governance, and mobile-first engagement still favor commercial platforms (Augmentir, Poka, Parsable, MaintainX, Tulip, Dozuki, Operations1, Beekeeper, WorkJam, etc.).

- Focusing open-source efforts on data ownership, cost control for smaller plants, and capturing tribal knowledge without lock-in.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Author procedures in an open wiki or form tool → link to assets via QR → deliver on tablets or wearables → capture completion and feedback → manage maintenance in an open or hybrid CMMS → improve content in closed-loop reviews. Suitable for plants with strong continuous-improvement culture and IT support. Many manufacturers still adopt commercial connected-frontline platforms for speed, usability, and scale.
